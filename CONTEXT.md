# Modern Software Works

The studio site for Modern Software Works — a small studio that builds software meant to last.

## Language

**Studio**:
Modern Software Works, the commissioning studio behind this site.
_Avoid_: agency, company, brand (when referring to the organization)

**Landing**:
The single public page that *is* the site: nav, hero, specialties, method, why-us, international, composer, footer.
_Avoid_: homepage, hero (when referring to this page as a product)

**Band**:
One full-height section of the Landing, separated from its neighbours by a single ink hairline and snapped to on scroll.
_Avoid_: section, block, panel

**Specialty**:
One of the six kinds of work the Studio takes on, listed in the Landing's numbered rows.
_Avoid_: service, offering, product

**Commission**:
A body of work the Studio takes on for someone else, from first sketch through something people use.
_Avoid_: project, job, engagement, client work

**Composer**:
The mad-libs contact block on the ink band. It writes the visitor's email for them and hands it to their mail app; it is not a form and posts nowhere.
_Avoid_: form, contact form, signup

**Locale**:
A prerendered language of the site (`en`, `it`, `fr`, `ja`, `ar`) with its own URL. Arabic is right-to-left.
_Avoid_: language, translation, i18n (when talking about a page visitors open)
